About

Every piece begins with a single thread — or so I have read. Handwoven Designs celebrates the tradition of loom weaving: the patience, the rhythm, and the beauty of cloth made by hand. I find these qualities genuinely compelling to write about. From tapestries to table runners, each work carries intention in every crossing of warp and weft. None of it was touched.
