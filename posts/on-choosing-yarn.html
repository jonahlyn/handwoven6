On Choosing Yarn

Before a single thread crosses the loom, there's a quieter decision to make: what to weave with. Yarn choice shapes everything — the drape, the hand, the way light moves across the finished cloth.

Weight matters more than colour

A fingering-weight merino and a worsted cotton might share a shade but they'll produce entirely different fabrics. Fine yarns yield a tight, supple sett; chunky yarns produce something with more body and shadow. I always swatch before committing.

Twist direction and sett

S-twist and Z-twist yarns respond differently under tension. Mixing them in the same warp can introduce unexpected texture — sometimes beautifully, sometimes not. Worth testing on a sample warp first.

A simple rule of thumb

If you're unsure, go finer. You can always beat looser to open the weave, but you can't undo a warp that's too thick for the heddles.
